Fam Feed — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

Fam Feed is a private meal-planning tool built for use within one household and the people it's shared with directly. This page explains what information the app handles.

What information is stored

When you use Fam Feed, the following is saved so the app can work and stay in sync across devices:

  • Names entered for household members (first names or nicknames — no accounts, emails, or passwords are collected)
  • Meal suggestions, votes, and planner assignments entered into the app
  • Shopping list items and archived shopping lists
  • Links pasted into the app (e.g. recipe URLs), and any content fetched from those links to help build a shopping list

Where it's stored

Data is stored in a Redis database connected to this app's hosting provider (Vercel) and is only accessible to people who have the private link to this specific household's copy of the app. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose beyond running the app.

Who can see it

Anyone with the app's link can view and edit the shared household data — there is no login system. The link is intended to be shared only with your own household and family, not posted publicly.

Third parties

When you use the "pull ingredients from a link" feature, the app makes a request to the web address you provide in order to read the recipe. No data about you personally is sent to that website beyond a standard web request.

Children

Fam Feed may be used by family members of any age within a household, under the supervision of the adult who set up the app. No information is knowingly collected from children beyond a first name or nickname they're identified by within their own family's private list.

Deleting your data

Any household member with access to the app can delete meal suggestions, shopping list items, or household member entries directly within the app at any time. To remove all data permanently, the app's owner can clear the underlying database or take the app offline.

Contact

Questions about this app can be directed to the household member who set it up and shared the link with you.
