🍽️ How Fam Feed works
  • 🍽️ Mains — anyone adds meal ideas here, sides live in their own library. No pressure, no voting yet — it's just the pool to pick from.
  • 📅 Planner — assign ideas to nights. Once a night has a meal on it, everyone can react with 😋 😐 🤢 — voting is on what's actually happening, not hypotheticals.
  • 🚀 / 🎲 Can't decide? — Quick Plan walks you through empty nights one at a time, or hit Spin and let it choose for you.
  • 🛒 Shopping List — builds itself from what you plan, merging duplicate ingredients automatically.
